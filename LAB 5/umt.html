About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public research university in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia, specializing in marine science, oceanography, fisheries, and aquatic resource management[cite: 6].

History: Started in 1979 as a small fisheries center. After a few name changes, it became an official university called Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) in 2007[cite: 6].

Location: Located by the coast in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, with nearby research stations[cite: 6].

Main Subjects: Focuses on marine science, oceans, fisheries, environmental studies, and business[cite: 6].
